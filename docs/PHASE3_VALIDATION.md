# Phase 3 historical validation — results

Generated by `python -m scripts.validate_history --symbols BTC ETH SOL --interval 1h --days 120`
against real Hyperliquid 1h candles (2026-05-19 → 2026-09-07, 2,681 replayed bars
per symbol), costs modelled at 5.5 bps per side (4.5 fee + 1.0 slippage).

## Headline

**The indicator weights do not hold up on real data.** They were tuned against
synthetic trends, and on that data they look excellent. On real market data the
analyst is worse than a coin flip, and confidence carries almost no information.

| | Synthetic trend | Real (BTC/ETH/SOL) |
|---|---|---|
| Trades | 23 | 1,124 |
| Hit rate (net of costs) | 100% | **34.0%** |
| Hit rate (gross) | 100% | 43.6% |
| Brier score | 0.015 | **0.401** |
| Expected calibration error | 0.117 | **0.402** |
| Overconfidence | −0.117 | **+0.402** |
| Profit factor | ∞ | 0.64 – 0.71 |

A Brier score of 0.25 is what a permanent 50/50 guess scores. At 0.401 the
analyst's stated confidences are worse than saying "I don't know" every time.

## Calibration is inverted, not merely miscalibrated

Aggregated across the three symbols:

| Stated confidence | n | Mean stated | Actual hit rate | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.40 – 0.60 | 237 | 0.574 | 0.346 | −0.228 |
| 0.60 – 0.80 | 537 | 0.691 | 0.313 | −0.378 |
| 0.80 – 1.00 | 350 | 0.935 | 0.377 | −0.558 |

The 0.80–1.00 bucket does not outperform the 0.40–0.60 bucket in any meaningful
way (0.377 vs 0.346), and on ETH the high-confidence bucket is *worse* (0.327 vs
0.357). Confidence is currently close to noise with respect to outcome.

This matters operationally: the risk manager sizes positions off confidence and
the decision engine gates on it. Sizing up on high confidence is, on this
evidence, sizing up on nothing.

## Two distinct problems

Separating gross from net isolates them:

1. **Little or no directional edge.** Gross hit rate is 42.8–46.2% with a
   slightly negative gross PnL. Roughly break-even before costs.
2. **Costs dominate.** Average holding period is 3–5 bars. At 11 bps round trip
   and ~1,100 trades, costs are the difference between "roughly flat" and
   "clearly losing". BTC: −1,873 gross becomes −23,334 net.

Problem 2 is arguably the more actionable: even a real edge of this size would be
consumed by turnover at this holding period.

## Why the synthetic tests missed it

`trending_candles` produces a monotone drift plus a sine wobble. A trend-following
scorer on a series that trends by construction is being asked a question whose
answer is in the premise. It scored 100% and Brier 0.015 — which validated
arithmetic, not edge.

The synthetic tests remain useful for exactly that: they check that RSI, MACD and
the rest compute correctly and that the sign conventions hold. They should never
have been read as evidence the strategy works.

## What this does not say

- One 4-month window on three correlated majors is a small sample. It is enough
  to reject "the weights are validated", not enough to conclude "no edge exists".
- Only the 1h timeframe was replayed. Multi-timeframe confluence — the analyst's
  main mechanism — is only partly exercised.
- No parameter search was run. These are the shipped defaults, not a tuned model.

## Suggested next steps

1. **Do not go live.** This configuration loses money on the data it has been
   tested against.
2. Fix calibration before anything else. `full_conviction_score` maps a raw
   composite onto confidence with a constant that was chosen so signals would
   clear the decision floor — not because it reflects any measured hit rate. Fit
   it to the realised buckets above instead.
3. Raise the bar for trading at all: at 34% net hit rate the decision floor of
   0.55 is admitting far too much. Test a much higher floor and accept far fewer
   trades.
4. Attack turnover directly — longer holds, wider stops, or a minimum expected
   move that exceeds round-trip costs before entry is allowed.
5. Re-run this validation after each change. `scripts/validate_history.py` is
   deterministic against the cached candles, so results are comparable run to run.

## Reproducing

```bash
.venv/Scripts/python -m scripts.validate_history --symbols BTC ETH SOL --interval 1h --days 120
```

Candles are cached under `data/history/`; pass `--refresh` to refetch from the
Hyperliquid REST API.
